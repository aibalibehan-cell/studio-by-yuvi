Who are We? About Us

Creative Excellence & Passion Genius Arts Story

Genius Arts Photography Studio is Tingre Nagar, Pune's premier destination for professional studio photography, outdoor cinematic videography, and customized gift printing.

We blend modern lighting setups with creative storytelling to capture emotions that last a lifetime. Over the past 10+ years, our studio has captured over 5,000+ shoots, serving families, couples, babies, and corporate brands with 98% client satisfaction.

From fast passport photos to grand weddings and high-end customized gift printing (magic mugs, t-shirts, mobile covers, photo framing, photo albums), we deliver excellence in every single frame.

Our deep commitment to creative artistry and client satisfaction ensures that every assignment stands the test of absolute quality and perfection.
